Privacy & data

Last updated: 4 November 2026

pipSmart is currently an educational forex practice website. It does not provide live market data, financial advice, broker connections, account registration or real-money trading.

What is stored

When you add or delete journal entries, pipSmart stores those entries in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. The current version does not send journal entries to a pipSmart server.

Your control

You can clear the saved journal by clearing this site's browser data. Doing so permanently removes locally stored entries. There is currently no cloud backup or account-based synchronisation.

Third-party services

The website may request font files from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google's servers to retrieve those fonts. The app does not currently include advertising or analytics tools.

Children and financial risk

pipSmart is educational content, not a recommendation to trade. Forex and leveraged products can cause rapid losses. Learn with a demo account and check the laws and protections that apply in your country before using any financial service.

Contact

A dedicated support contact will be added before a public app-store release.

This is a starter privacy notice for the current no-account, local-storage version. It must be reviewed and updated before app-store publication, especially if analytics, accounts, cloud sync or other data collection are added.
